### **Content Flow (由上而下)**

#### **1. [Image] 模型與軸測圖 (Model & Axon)**

**(Filename: `Untitled-2-05`)**

This project explores the dialectic of light and enclosure through a "box" situated
between interior and exterior. Inspired by the duality of a mask—concealing
while defining a face—the architecture functions as a mechanical apparatus.
It begins as a sealed darkroom. Through the unfolding of its skin, the darkroom
breathes, transforming from a hermetic void into a theater of exposure. The
opening of the pivot door is not merely a functional act but a spatial event: the
black interior expands to claim the exterior, while natural light penetrates to
rewrite the room's atmosphere.


#### **2. [Image] 電影蒙太奇 (Cinematic Montage)**

**(Filename: `Untitled-2-02`)**

> **Cinematic Reference: The Terrorizers (1986)**
> Inspired by Edward Yang's depiction of Taipei's urban solitude, specifically the photographer's darkroom . The design translates the film's atmospheric tension—the drifting photographs, the sudden intrusion of light upon curtain opening, and the shifting boundaries between observer and observed—into an architectural mechanism.

#### **4. [Image] 剖面圖 (Section)**

**(Filename: `Untitled-2-04`)**

The Pivot & The Platform
Two kinetic elements renegotiate the boundary between darkroom and platform.
The Pivot Door functions as a mask; its rotation is an act of "unmasking," projecting
the black interior into the white exterior. The Unfolding Platform breaks the hermetic
seal, extending the floor to create a semi-outdoor interface. Sharing identical
geometric logic in plan and section—a rotating quarter-circle void—the design
operates as a coherent machine for light control.
